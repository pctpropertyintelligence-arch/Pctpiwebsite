About PCT

A modern, independent property intelligence business.

PCT Property Intelligence was built around a simple idea: property owners deserve clear, well-documented information about the properties they own, manage or let — not just a verbal opinion and a handful of photographs.

Photograph: PCT at work on site
Our Story

Practical property knowledge, combined with modern technology.

PCT Property Intelligence is a new UK property-services and property-intelligence business, built around professional property assessment, inspection, energy information and detailed visual reporting.

The business is being developed deliberately and transparently: services are added as the right equipment, qualifications and processes are in place, rather than promised before they exist. What's available today is genuinely available. What's coming is clearly marked as coming soon.

The result is a company built on practical property knowledge, careful documentation and modern inspection technology — used to give customers better information about the property in front of them, not to impress with gadgets.

What We Believe

The principles behind PCT Property Intelligence

Show, don't just tell

Photographic and visual evidence, not just a verbal summary of what was found.

Clear, structured reporting

Observations and recommendations are always kept separate and clearly labelled.

Honest about capability

We only advertise services, equipment and qualifications we actually have.

Property first, technology second

Drones, mapping and digital reporting exist to serve better information — not the other way around.

Today

Inspection, documentation & energy assessment

Drone inspection, roof & gutter inspection, photography, and Domestic Energy Assessment/EPC from an ecmk-accredited assessor.

Next

Retrofit assessment & gutter cleaning

Retrofit Assessor qualification, plus professional gutter cleaning as our equipment is finalised.

Future

Digital property intelligence platform

A secure client portal holding every report, photo and record for your property.

Where We're Heading

Building toward a broader property intelligence platform

PCT Property Intelligence is designed from day one to grow into more than a single inspection visit — eventually combining assessment, energy information, drone imagery, mapping and reporting into one ongoing digital property record.
