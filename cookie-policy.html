Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains how PCT Property Intelligence uses cookies on this website, and how you can manage your preferences.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function and, where permitted, help us understand how the site is used.

Cookies we use

TypePurposeConsent required?
EssentialRemembering your cookie preference, and basic site functionality.No — required for the site to work.
AnalyticsUnderstanding how visitors use the site, so we can improve it.Yes — only loaded after you accept.

Managing your preference

When you first visit, you'll be asked whether to accept analytics cookies. Analytics cookies are not loaded until you accept them. You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this website, which will show the preference banner again.

Third-party cookies

Where analytics are enabled, they may be provided by a third-party analytics service. We only use analytics data in aggregate to understand site usage — not to identify individuals.

More information

See our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal information more broadly, or contact us at info@pctpropertyintelligence.co.uk.
