What is Property Intelligence?

Your property has a story. We help you understand it.

Most property owners have information scattered across EPCs, old photographs, contractor notes, maintenance records and inspection reports — if they have it at all. PCT Property Intelligence brings this together into a clearer, structured picture of your property, so you can see its condition, performance and history in one place.
