How often should gutters be cleaned?

PCT Property Intelligence·Roof & Gutter
How often should gutters be cleaned?

Gutters do a quiet but important job: moving rainwater safely away from your roof, walls and foundations. When they're blocked, that water has to go somewhere else — often down external walls, into fascias, or pooling around foundations. The frustrating part is that a blocked gutter often isn't visible from ground level, so problems can build up unnoticed.

How often should gutters actually be cleaned?

As a general guide, most UK properties benefit from a gutter check at least twice a year — typically in spring and autumn. That said, the right frequency depends heavily on what's around the property:

  • Properties near trees, especially overhanging deciduous trees, can need checking every few months during autumn leaf fall.
  • Properties with moss-prone roof coverings may see gutters fill with moss debris even without nearby trees.
  • Properties in more sheltered, tree-free locations may comfortably manage with an annual check.

Signs your gutters may need attention

  • Visible sagging or pooling water in sections of the gutter.
  • Plant growth or moss visible from ground level.
  • Water marks or staining on external walls beneath the gutter line.
  • Overflow during heavy rain, even where the gutter looks intact.

Why inspection matters as much as cleaning

A clean gutter today doesn't tell you much about what it will look like in six months. That's why we treat inspection and cleaning as two separate, connected steps: inspection identifies blockages, moss, debris, overflow risk and any visible defects; cleaning — where suitable equipment is available — then addresses what's found. Just as importantly, we document the result with photographs, so you're shown the outcome rather than simply told your gutters are clear.
