Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PCT Property Intelligence ("PCT", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

PCT Property Intelligence is a property assessment, inspection and intelligence business operating in Worcestershire and surrounding areas. You can contact us about privacy matters at info@pctpropertyintelligence.co.uk.

What information we collect

  • Enquiry and quote form details — name, email, telephone number, property address and postcode, service requirements, preferred dates, and any details you choose to add.
  • Photographs you choose to send us relating to your property or enquiry.
  • Basic technical information — such as pages visited and general usage patterns, only where you have consented to analytics cookies. See our Cookie Policy.

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide quotes.
  • To arrange, carry out and document assessments and inspections you book.
  • To keep records associated with your property, where you become a customer.
  • To meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

Legal basis for processing

We process your information on the basis of: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (responding to enquiries and quotes); performing a contract with you (carrying out booked work); and our legitimate interests in running and improving our business, balanced against your rights.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate (for example, email or hosting providers), and where required by law.

How long we keep your information

We keep enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to it, and customer and property records for as long as necessary to provide our services and meet legal obligations.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@pctpropertyintelligence.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website uses cookies. See our Cookie Policy for full details and how to manage your preferences.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
