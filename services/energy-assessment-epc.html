Domestic Energy Assessment & EPC

A Domestic Energy Assessment produces the Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) that tells you, your buyer or your tenant how efficiently a property uses energy. PCT's assessments are carried out by an ABBE-qualified Domestic Energy Assessor, and the energy information sits alongside every other piece of property information in one place.

Available now
What's Involved

How this service works

A full internal and external surveyMeasuring and recording construction, insulation, heating, hot water, glazing and lighting.
A standard energy calculationProducing the EPC energy efficiency rating (A–G) using the approved methodology.
A lodged, valid certificateRegistered on the official government EPC register.
What You Receive

Clear, usable outputs

Your property's EPC rating

A clear A–G energy efficiency rating.

Improvement suggestions

Practical recommendations to improve efficiency.

A lodged certificate

Valid for the standard 10-year period.
